Research

Confidence estimation for text-to-SQL. Can we tell whether an LLM-generated SQL query is correct without a reference answer? We compare black-box methods, based on consistency across sampled queries, with white-box methods that use token probabilities in a SQL-aware way. Our SQL-aware method improves AUC-ROC by up to 12% and reduces expected calibration error by up to 16% on BIRD, and grounding queries in their execution results improves both families of methods. Published at AAAI 2026.
Auditing column errors. A wrong column in a generated query is not always the model’s fault. We built a pipeline that separates errors caused by ambiguity in the database schema from errors caused by ambiguous questions and from genuine model errors, using multiple generations per question, LLM judges, and human annotation on the BIRD benchmark. We find that most column substitutions made by one LLM on BIRD come from ambiguity rather than from the model, consistently across judges from three model families and in agreement with human annotators. Under review.
Detecting schema ambiguity without questions. We are developing a method that finds column pairs storing the same information by testing transformations over the data, and comparing it with embedding-based and LLM-based detectors. In preparation.

Across these projects, I run and fine-tune open-source LLMs on the GPU clusters of the Digital Research Alliance of Canada (Compute Canada).
